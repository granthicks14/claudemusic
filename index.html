Beat Studio

Pick a style, build a full arrangement, then produce it your way.

Naming an artist describes the style — it is the standard type-beat convention and does not imply endorsement. It is not a licence: credit never grants permission to use anyone's recording. Nothing here needs one, because every beat is composed from scratch by this program and contains no sampled audio.

    Open on YouTube ↗

    The link opens the real recording on YouTube in a new tab. Nothing is downloaded, streamed or sampled — the program only takes the tempo, key and genre, and those are facts, not property. What you get is an original beat that sits in the same pocket as a record you like. Tempo and key are reference values and every control stays editable.

    Your file is read in the browser and never uploaded anywhere. Tempo and key are detected and the generated beat is matched to them. Centre reduction is not source separation — a centred lead vocal largely cancels, but so does anything else centred (usually kick and bass, which are partly restored), and stereo reverb on the vocal stays. Use it on audio you own or that is licensed for it — ccMixter exists precisely for this.

    Every term is a measurement, not an opinion, and each one is weighted. The score is the weighted mean of all of them: 100 · Σwᵢgᵢ(xᵢ) / Σwᵢ. Terms with a target band use a Gaussian tolerance curve, so being under is penalised the same way as being over.

    Drag right on a melodic lane to draw a note and set its length; drag across a drum lane to paint hits. Click any note or hit to delete it. Click a track name to open its piano roll for exact pitches. Each track's R knob controls its reverb send.

    Click to add a volume point, drag a point to move it, click a point to delete it. This shapes the track's level over the whole arrangement.
